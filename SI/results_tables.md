# SI — Results Compendium / SCEM Paper 2 §5.5 External Validation (GSS + ESS)

**Version:** 1.0 (2026-07-01) / **Parent:** `SI/README.md` (three-way reproduction table)
**Purpose:** for writing the §5.5 body, aggregate **all GSS/ESS studies and their real numbers on a single page.** Each value is transcribed from a committed output file (`data/{gss,ess}_results/`). Referenced from body and figures by study ID (S1–S11).

**Strength wording (common to all studies, strict):** never write "**confirmation**." Use "**prediction-consistent / suggestive / modest / validity of the tendency bundle**." Sensitivity-robustness = operationalization-independence, not strong evidence. **The validation question** = not predictive accuracy but a validity check of "the existence of an intermediate tendency bundle that clears the personalization ↔ statistical-aggregate trade-off" (spec 0.3 §0).

**Main finding (two-tier structure):** community premise drives the **level**, `effective_year` drives **slope visibility (transition stage)**.

## S5. Guns = observation instrument × event time-structure mismatch (EVENT_STRUCTURE)
Output: `overlay_summary.json` (`event_structure`)

| Type | Example | Birth-year slope | GSS (birth-year × attitude) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single moment | Obergefell 2015 / Dobbs 2022 | appears | visible |
| Repeated burst | mass shootings Columbine→Uvalde | in principle does not appear | not visible |

Mass shootings = repeated → every birth year is hit within its sensitivity window → no birth-year difference. GUNLAW is saturated above 70% and not contested. **The grid's guns = ACTIVE prediction is valid; GSS simply cannot image repeated events** (not a grid failure but an instrument mismatch). Lesson: when measurement fails, first ask "do the instrument and the event's time structure mesh?" before grinding on with another variable.

# Part C. Two-tier structure (cross-national integration) and caveats

## C1. Two-tier structure (core / conclusion of §5.5.3)
| Tier | Driver | Content | Universality | Empirical strength | Main studies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1: **level gate** | community premise | secular/educated/urban high, religious/low-edu low | **universal** (US/EU, 3 pillars) | strong (direction settled) | S1, S6, S10 |
| Tier 2: **slope visibility** | effective_year | b′ is visible only in mid-transition communities; complete/not-started are flat | transition-stage-dependent | suggestive | S7, S8, S9 |

= An intermediate structure invisible to the bucket (birth-year only) stood up across countries via premise × effective_year × cohort, without requiring personalization (= validity of clearing the trade-off). Tier 2 is a real-data glimpse of Paper 3's (c) temporal evolution.

## C2. List of caveats (do not hide; keep in the body too)
- GSS b′ interaction p=0.16 (single flagship, n.s.) → recovered by the two-tier structure (S2).
- overlay base-rate inflation (discriminative power is on the grid-ACTIVE → transition side) (S4, S11).
- grid REFRAME premature judgment (SSM-Suburban / abortion-Coastal) (S4) → Paper 3.
- ESS near-saturation unstable slopes (overfitting artifacts, improvement 0) → do not use for interpretation (S7).
- effective_year ρ moderate, n=30, GR/CY 2 waves, Eastern-European noise (S9). Institutional years still need a final cross-check against the ILGA Rainbow Map.
- ESS is an external cross-check that does not directly measure subjective mode (Prolific = Paper 3).
